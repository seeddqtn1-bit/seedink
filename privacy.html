Privacy Policy

Ink Uploader · Effective 17 November 2026 · Home

1. What Ink Uploader is

Ink Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Ink Encyclopedia YouTube channels. It runs on the owner's own computer and connects to the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, only after the owner signs in with Google and grants permission. It is not a public service: it does not offer accounts and does not access anyone else's YouTube or Google account.

2. Data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Ink Uploader accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels:

  • channel identity (channel ID, title, handle);
  • the owner's own videos and their details (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, privacy and publish time, playlist membership), in order to upload, schedule and update them;
  • view statistics of the owner's own channel and videos (YouTube Analytics), in order to report how they perform.

It does not read other users' data, private messages, contacts, payment information or anything outside YouTube.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests: uploading his videos, setting their details, scheduling them, and reading his channel statistics. The data is not sold, not shared with or transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Where the data is stored

The permission token and a short log of uploads are stored only on the owner's computer. Ink Uploader has no server and no database, and does not send the data anywhere except to Google's YouTube APIs.

5. Google API Services and YouTube

Ink Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Ink Uploader uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (choose the app, then "Remove access"). Revoking access makes the stored token useless; the owner can also delete the token file from his computer.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Ink Encyclopedia YouTube channel: youtube.com/@InkEncyclopedia.

Tóm tắt tiếng Việt

Ink Uploader là công cụ riêng của chủ kênh Ink Encyclopedia, chạy trên máy của chủ kênh. Với sự cho phép của chủ kênh, công cụ chỉ truy cập kênh YouTube của chính chủ kênh, để tải lên, hẹn giờ, sửa thông tin video và đọc số liệu. Dữ liệu chỉ lưu trên máy chủ kênh, không bán, không chia sẻ cho bên thứ ba, không dùng cho quảng cáo hay huấn luyện AI. Thu hồi quyền bất cứ lúc nào tại myaccount.google.com/permissions.
